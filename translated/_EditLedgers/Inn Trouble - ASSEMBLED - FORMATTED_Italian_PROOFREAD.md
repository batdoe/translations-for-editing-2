## Dialogue punctuation (ruling 1): 213 paragraphs APPLIED
The dossier caporali system is now uniform:
- `«…», disse` and `«…,» disse` → `«…» disse` (tag outside, no comma).
- `«…?».` / `«….».` → `«…?»` / `«….»`.
- `«…». ` → `«….» ` (final period inside when no tag follows).
- Interruptions `...»` → `—»`, matching the EN em dash [40][53].
- Ellipses → `…` [89][720].
- Quoted phrases inside narration → "…" [204][429].

The edition had no em-dash dialogue.
