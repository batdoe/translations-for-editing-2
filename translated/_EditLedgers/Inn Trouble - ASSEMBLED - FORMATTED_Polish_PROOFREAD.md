## Headline
**Worst defect:** the blackmail NOTE and its key clue were left in ENGLISH. Ch2 [185] printed the whole note in English ("I know how your family came by this inn… the sapphire at its heart…") and [187] the clue line "The sapphire at its heart"; the rest of the book used three other wordings ("Szafir w jej sercu", "Szafirem w sercu", "(ze szafirem w sercu)" on Theo's card), so the Ch19 match between the note and Theo's catalogue card — the deduction that convicts him — did not exist in Polish. Now one wording everywhere: **„szafir w jej sercu”** (note, Jessica's readings, the card, Ch19, Ch22 recap, Ch23, the confrontation), and every "five words" claim changed to **cztery słowa** (the Polish phrase has 4).

Second: **note vs. the 1924 letters merged.** The blackmail note was called `list/listy` ×22 (the word for the Blackburn letters) — incl. Ch19 [872] "napisał listy terroryzujące Fionę" (said Theo wrote the LETTERS), Ch26 confrontation [1153] "Listy z szantażem" and [1183] "Ale listy wiedziały…" (followed by "…in no Blackburn letter"). Now the note is always `liścik` / `notatka` / `wiadomość`, and `list/listy` is reserved for the 1924 letters (and ordinary mail).

Other critical classes: reversed meanings (blurb made Sam the debtor; Ch8 "mieć u kogoś dług" again made Sam owe; Ch6 "crossed my heart" as crossing fingers; Ch14 "a dry fountain is of use to a penguin"; Ch4 "whom the rich families belonged to"; Ch25 "it IS a misunderstanding I can wave off"); a 1094-for-1924 date (Ch9); Constance confused with Fiona (Ch9 "do drzwi Fiony Okafor"); MT debris printed in the prose (Ch4 "podeszed… podeszł… ×6", "Pocat… Pocal…", Theo's speech self-correction; Ch18 "powiedziałaś..."; Ch22 "Oczyszczono mnie… a raczej to ja oczyściłam…"; Ch25 "Wetrzeć twarz płaską dłonią nie sposób, ale…"; Ch29 "patrzą… patrzel…"; Ch31 English "perishable"); a Cyrillic "Не" (Ch2); Czech spelling "przestępstva"; gender errors on Theo/Neil/Nick/Jessica ×21; Pip declined as "Pipa/Pipy/Pipo/Pipę" ×12; cider = alcoholic "cydr" ×11.
