## Worst defect: the Book-3 clue chain was broken
Joyce's giveaway word "the cold room" was translated as «la cella frigorifera». That is the same word used for the walk-in.
- Greta's key speech, Ch20 [1307][1309]: «Joyce la chiamava la cella frigorifera… Dicevo cella».
- The confrontation, Ch23 [1487]: the complaint "called her walk-in the cold room" became «ha definito la sua cella frigorifera una cella».
- Joyce's defence, Ch23 [1489]: «Metà dei fornai della mia età dice cella frigorifera».

As a result, the slip that convicts Joyce disappeared from the chapter that turns on it. Two more places had the same problem:
- Ch22 used a third rendering, «la stanza fredda».
- Ch24 used the walk-in's name again.

Fix: the walk-in is now always «cella frigorifera», and Joyce's word is always «la camera fredda». This holds in the complaint (Ch9), Greta's speech (Ch20), the case (Ch22), the confrontation (Ch23) and the charges (Ch24).
