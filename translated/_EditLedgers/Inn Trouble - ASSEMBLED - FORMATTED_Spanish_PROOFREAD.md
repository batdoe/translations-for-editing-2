### ES2-D1. Translator-added scene breaks — [1178] (Ch25) and [1348] (Ch30)
`* * *` is in the Spanish where the English has no break: in Ch25 between "It was right…" and "Then I went to Fiona…", and in Ch30 between the frost on the elm and "A moment later Bram…".
