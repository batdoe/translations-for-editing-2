## Headline
Worst defect: the clue word **sour base** was rendered **"maia"** (= sourdough *starter*, the very thing Doc says the powder replaces — Ch11 "fără maia") in Ch21 (Greta's supplier question, Jessica's call to Tanaka, Pelkey's account) and Ch24 (both charges), and "bază acrită / bază de acrit" in Ch17/Ch22, so the Pelkey's-invoice proof no longer named the substance Doc found. Unified to **bază acră** everywhere. Joyce's giveaway pair is intact and distinct (walk-in = **camera frigorifică**, her word = **„camera rece”**), but the hand-sink had become a *dish* sink (Ch22) and a "service sink" (Ch16); unified to **chiuveta de spălat mâini** vs **„chiuveta Hobart”**.

Other critical classes:
- **The whole back matter was English**: both sell lines, "What's Next?", the blurb, "Excerpt from", the 50-paragraph excerpt and the closing CTA — with the sell lines and CTA wrapped *entirely* in `<bt>…</bt>`. Translated; excerpt replaced verbatim from the published Romanian edition.
