1. **Walk-in vs Joyce's "cold room" erased, then reversed.** Varney's key line, Ch9 [423]: EN "Called the walk-in 'the cold room'" → DE `Sie nannte den Kühlraum ›den Kühlraum‹` (the slip vanished).
