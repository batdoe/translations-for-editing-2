## Inn Trouble - ASSEMBLED - FORMATTED_Filipino — PROOFCHECK (2026-10-28)

### PC-FIL2-1. The back-matter excerpt (Flour Play Ch1, paras 1500–1547) is a separate translation, not the corrected Flour Play's text. OPEN
- **What's there:** all 48 excerpt paragraphs line up 1:1 with corrected Flour Play Filipino Ch1 (`translated/corrected/Flour Play - ASSEMBLED - FORMATTED_Filipino.docx`, paras 39–134). They are a different, less polished rendering of the same MT. Examples:
  - [1500] `Ang bawat babae sa bilog ay nagdala ng kanyang sariling balsam… Inilagay ko ang alambre sa kuwadro…`. Flour Play has `…nagdala ng sarili niyang sanga ng balsam… Inilapat ko nang dahan-dahan ang alambre sa balangkas…`.
  - [1535] `“Ngayon kakaiba iyon,”` (a literal calque of "Now that's odd"). Flour Play has `“Kakaiba iyon,”`.
  - [1536] `Ang liwasan ay nakaupo sa tapat ng Yarn Tales…`. Flour Play has `Ang liwasan ay nasa tapat ng Yarn Tales…`.
- **Already fixed here, because it is wrong against both English texts:** [1547] `Itinali ko ang balsam pauwi` ("bound the balsam *homeward*") → `Itinali ko ang balsam`, which is Flour Play's wording.
- **Why it can't be settled automatically:** the English is not the same in the two books. Inn Trouble's English excerpt is an older draft of Flour Play Ch1. In 22 of the 48 paragraphs the current Flour Play English differs from it, several by only a word. The bigger differences:
  - The green has "the war memorial near the top and the great elm at the far end" in Flour Play, but "the bandstand at one end and the war memorial at the other" in Inn Trouble.
  - Flour Play has "the Halloween snow", "widowed not yet a year" and "the long blue shadow of the war memorial". Inn Trouble has none of these.
  - Several Inn Trouble sentences were cut from Flour Play: "and thought that the magic… had a nose for what mattered", "And I didn't say what I was thinking", "Something was starting", and "No name on the permit board yet either, I checked".

  If the corrected Flour Play text is copied in, the excerpt will match the book as sold, but it will no longer match Inn Trouble's own English master.
- **Clue checks:** both versions already agree on these. "Very nearly" = `“Halos na halos.”` (correct, not "absolutely"). The circle = `bilog`, the green = `liwasan`, Snowball = `Snowball`, snow = `niyebe`, and the units (`anim na pulgada`) follow FIL2-D3.
- **Options:**
  - (a) Copy corrected Flour Play Ch1 into [1500]–[1547] word for word, 48 paragraphs to 48 with no change to the paragraph count. Recommended: the excerpt then reads exactly like the book it sells. It would also be worth updating the English Inn Trouble master's excerpt to the current Flour Play Ch1.
  - (b) Keep the current excerpt. It is faithful to Inn Trouble's older English, and only the one-answer fix above has been applied.
