## Headline
Worst defect: Joyce's giveaway word "the cold room" — the Ch20/22/23 deduction — was rendered five different ways ("pomieszczenie chłodnicze", "chłodnia", "chłodne pomieszczenie", "komora chłodnicza", "pomieszczenie gospodarcze"), and in Ch20 it was SWAPPED with the walk-in word (walk-in = "mroźnia", cold room = "chłodnia"), so the slip that convicts Joyce no longer existed. Now: walk-in = **chłodnia**, cold room = **„zimny pokój”**, hand-sink = **zlew umywalkowy**, Hobart sink = **zlew Hobarta**, identical at every occurrence.

Other critical classes: machine-translation debris printed in the prose (Ch14 "Odeszła od larsu... zaszła ladę", "His... Jego matka", "karm... karmiła"; Ch22 "dwugodzinnego... dwudziestego siódmego" inside the key date sentence; Ch24 Tanaka "wylegitymowałem... to znaczy wylegitymowałam – ucięła, poprawiając się w duchu"); foreign words ("não" PT, "weil" DE, "Nhưng" VI, "Niedbalstvo" CZ); a whole English line of Renée's dialogue (Ch17 [1136]); sour base rendered as zakwas / rozczyn / zaczyn / kwaśne ciasto / kwas bazowy / baza zakwasowa (sourdough words) — unified to **kwaśna baza**; order book in four renderings — unified to **zeszyt zamówień**; DIRTY rendered as the vulgarity "SRAKA" (Ch14); Pip rendered male ×11 and Jessica/Danny/Pete/Varney/Hal wrong gender ×15; reversed meanings ×7 (e.g. Ch10 Anne would NOT spend the good tea; Ch14 "zachowaj to dla siebie" = keep it secret, against "bring it to me"; Ch26 the children built "many" snowmen, killing the magic); one "poison" word not in the English (Ch16 "trucizny").
