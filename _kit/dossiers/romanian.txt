DOSSIER — ROMANIAN (Română)
Isabella Proctor paranormal cozy mystery series.
Read with TRANSLATOR_CORE.txt — the completeness mandate and "do not improve" rule bind here too.

NARRATIVE TENSE (LOCKED — must be IDENTICAL across every chapter of a book)
- First mirror the SOURCE: if the book narrates in the present, narrate in the present; if in the
  past (the usual case), narrate in the past. Never change a book's basic tense.
- For PAST-TENSE narration, use the tense named below and keep it identical in EVERY chapter.
  Mixing narrative tenses across chapters is a visible continuity error a reader WILL notice.
- ROMANIAN: perfect compus + imperfect for narration ('am mers'). NEVER the perfect simplu (regional/
  archaic) for narration.

REGISTER & VOICE
- Contemporary standard Romanian (limba română standardă): colloquial but literate, natural and pleasurable —
  neither stiff nor overly casual. Avoid regional dialect unless a character is written to speak one.
- Restructure sentences for natural Romanian rhythm, not word-for-word.
- Diminutives create warmth: căsuță (little house), cățeluș (little dog). Use as a Romanian writer would.
- Calibration: "căldura casei" (warmth of home) for cozy scenes; "haz de necaz" (laughing at one's troubles)
  is the genre register — wry laughter at the absurd/inconvenient, never despair. Touchstone: "Aparențele înșeală."
- Use correct Romanian diacritics throughout: ă â î ș ț.

HUMOR
- Ironic, self-deprecating, wry. The paranormal is an inconvenience endured with a sigh and a wry comment.
- Romanian understatement is warm and communal — less detached than British understatement.

DIALOGUE PUNCTUATION (critical — follow exactly)
- Direct speech: an em dash (—) opens each spoken line.
    EN: She said, "Good morning."                 ->  — Bună dimineața, spuse ea.
    EN: "Where are you going?" she asked.          ->  — Unde te duci? întrebă ea.
    EN: "I don't know," he said. "It's strange."   ->  — Nu știu, spuse el. E ciudat.
  Mid-line attribution: a second em dash closes, a third opens the continuation:
    EN: "Wait," she said, "I need to think."       ->  — Așteaptă — spuse ea — trebuie să mă gândesc.
  Interruption:  "Wait, I need to—" she began.     ->  — Așteaptă, trebuie să— începu ea.
- Quoted speech inside narration (reported, not direct dialogue): „ … " (low-9 open, high-6 close).
    EN: described it as "completely normal."        ->  O descrisese drept „cu totul normală".
- Question mark ? right after the last word (Unde? not Unde ?). Ellipsis … . Em dash — spaced for asides.
- Never begin a narration-only paragraph with a dialogue dash.

PARANORMAL TERMS
- ghost / spirit: fantomă (phantom), duh (warmer, colloquial)
- undead / revenant: strigoi (folkloric; this is Dracula's homeland — paranormal matter-of-factness resonates)
- witch: vrăjitoare (standard), babă (folkloric)
- haunted: bântuit / bântuită; "în care bântuie"
- spell / curse: vrajă, farmec (charm), blestem (curse)
- psychic / intuition: presimțire, medium, ghicitoare
- omen: semn, presemn, profeție
- lighter spirits: iele; darker folkloric flavor: Muma-Pădurii

IDIOMS (find the Romanian equivalent — never literal)
- spill the beans -> a da cărțile pe față / a divulga secretul
- on edge / nervous -> pe ghimpi / ca pe ace
- the plot thickens -> lucrurile se complică / misterul se adâncește
- gut feeling -> presimțire / instinct
- things aren't what they seem -> aparențele înșeală

COZY VOCABULARY
- cozy / welcoming: primitor/primitoare, confortabil, călduros/ă
- community: comunitate, vecini, mahala (affectionate/nostalgic)
- gossip: bârfă, a bârfi, bârfitor/bârfitoare
