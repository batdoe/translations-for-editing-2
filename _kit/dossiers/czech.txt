DOSSIER — CZECH (Čeština)
Isabella Proctor paranormal cozy mystery series.
Read with TRANSLATOR_CORE.txt — the completeness mandate and "do not improve" rule bind here too.

NARRATIVE TENSE (LOCKED — must be IDENTICAL across every chapter of a book)
- First mirror the SOURCE: if the book narrates in the present, narrate in the present; if in the
  past (the usual case), narrate in the past. Never change a book's basic tense.
- For PAST-TENSE narration, use the tense named below and keep it identical in EVERY chapter.
  Mixing narrative tenses across chapters is a visible continuity error a reader WILL notice.
- CZECH: minulý čas for narration; keep verbal ASPECT (dokonavý/nedokonavý) and register consistent
  across chapters. Do not switch to a historic present unless the source does.

REGISTER & VOICE
- Hovorová čeština (colloquial Czech) for narration and dialogue — a well-read person speaking naturally,
  not officialese. Avoid archaisms and bureaucratic phrasing.
- Prefer shorter, active sentences in natural speech rhythm. Drop pronouns where Czech naturally would
  (don't add them just because English has them). Use flexible, case-driven word order for emphasis.
- Diminutives (zdrobněliny) are the warmth engine: dům -> domeček, kočka -> kočička. Natural where English
  uses "little/dear" — this is normal Czech, not babyish.
- Calibration word: pohoda (comfortable, laid-back ease). If a scene has pohoda — slippers, tea, stay a while —
  you're on track.

HUMOR
- Dry irony, understatement, mild absurdism (Švejk/Havel tradition); keep to the light end.
- Understate: "docela nepříjemné" (rather unpleasant) where English shouts "completely terrifying."
- Deadpan acceptance of the paranormal, like a broken dishwasher. Use exclamation marks sparingly.

DIALOGUE PUNCTUATION (critical — follow exactly)
- Czech quotes: open „ (low-9, before the first word), close “ (high-6, after the final punctuation).
  The attribution comma sits INSIDE the closing quote.
    EN: She said, "Good morning."                 ->  „Dobré ráno,“ řekla.
    EN: "Where are you going?" he asked.           ->  „Kam jdeš?“ zeptal se.
    EN: "I don't know," she said. "It's strange."  ->  „Nevím,“ řekla. „Je to divné.“
- Interruption: em dash —  ("Počkej, potřebuji—"). Never a hyphen.
- Nested quote inside dialogue: single low-9/high-6 ‚ ‘  ("Hned mi řekla, ‚je tam strašidlo‘, od začátku.“).
- Ellipsis … (single char, no space before). Em dash — spaced for parenthetical asides.
  Comma before "a" (and) only when clarity needs it, not mechanically.
- Never begin a narration-only paragraph with a dialogue quote.

PARANORMAL TERMS
- ghost / spirit: duch (everyday), přízrak (literary apparition), zjev
- witch: čarodějnice (standard), ježibaba (folkloric hag), čarodějka (gentler, modern)
- haunted: strašidelný; "tam straší"
- spell / curse: kouzlo (spell/charm), kletba (curse), čáry (witchcraft)
- psychic / intuition: tušení, předtucha; jasnovidec (clairvoyant)
- omen: předznamenání, znamení
- Tone: nonchalant ("Bývali jsme se svou čarodějnicí deset let. Nikdy problém.")
