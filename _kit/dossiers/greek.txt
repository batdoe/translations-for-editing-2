DOSSIER — GREEK (Ελληνικά)
Isabella Proctor paranormal cozy mystery series.
Read with TRANSLATOR_CORE.txt — the completeness mandate and "do not improve" rule bind here too.

NARRATIVE TENSE (LOCKED — must be IDENTICAL across every chapter of a book)
- First mirror the SOURCE: if the book narrates in the present, narrate in the present; if in the
  past (the usual case), narrate in the past. Never change a book's basic tense.
- For PAST-TENSE narration, use the tense named below and keep it identical in EVERY chapter.
  Mixing narrative tenses across chapters is a visible continuity error a reader WILL notice.
- GREEK: αόριστος + παρατατικός for narration, kept consistent. Do not drift into the historic present
  unless the source itself does.

REGISTER & VOICE
- Standard modern demotic Greek (δημοτική). Avoid formal/archaic καθαρεύουσα. A warm, natural narrator — not a government form.
- Greek is naturally expressive and warm; match it without tipping into melodrama.
- Diminutives are natural and affectionate: σπιτάκι (little house), γατούλα (little cat). Use freely.
- Draw on the vocabulary of hospitality (φιλοξενία) and community for cozy scenes.
- Calibration: γειτονιά (the neighborhood that always notices, knows, and brings food) and κουβέντα
  (warm, slightly nosy chitchat — the social glue). If gossip/concern scenes feel like κουβέντα, you're on track.

HUMOR
- Ironic, self-aware, with warm hyperbole. Ironic acceptance of the absurd: "Ναι, το φάντασμα. Πάλι. Τέτοια μοίρα."
- Grumbling-with-affection is a form of intimacy in Greek. A moderate amount of hyperbole is authentic.

DIALOGUE PUNCTUATION (critical — follow exactly)
- Dialogue uses guillemets: open « , close » , no space inside the marks.
    EN: She said, "Good morning."                 ->  «Καλημέρα,» είπε.
    EN: "I don't know," he said. "It's strange."  ->  «Δεν ξέρω,» είπε. «Είναι παράξενο.»
- ! THE GREEK QUESTION MARK IS A SEMICOLON (;), NOT "?". Single most important rule.
    EN: "Where are you going?"  ->  «Πού πας;»
- Exclamation: ! (as English). Ellipsis: … . Em dash — for interruptions/asides, with spaces.
  Άνω τελεία (·) is rare — only for a weighty pause.
- Never begin a narration-only paragraph with a dialogue guillemet.

PARANORMAL TERMS
- ghost / spirit: φάντασμα (common), πνεύμα
- place-bound haunting spirit: στοιχειό (uniquely Greek)
- witch: μάγισσα (standard), στρίγλα (folkloric hag)
- haunted: στοιχειωμένο / στοιχειωμένη
- spell / curse: ξόρκι (spell), μάγια (magic), κατάρα (curse)
- psychic / intuition: ένστικτο, μέντιουμ
- folkloric-dark revenant: βρυκόλακας
- Tone: casual ("Αρχίσαμε να ζούμε με τα φαντάσματα σαν απλούς ενοικιαστές.")

IDIOMS (find the Greek equivalent — never literal)
- spill the beans -> τα είπε όλα / τα ξεχύλησε όλα
- on edge / nervous -> στα κάρβουνα / νευρικός
- the plot thickens -> τα πράγματα μπλέκονται
- gut feeling -> ένστικτο / αίσθημα στο στομάχι
- things aren't what they seem -> τα φαινόμενα απατούν
- bite the bullet -> σφίγγω τα δόντια
- works like magic -> σαν μαγεία

COZY VOCABULARY
- cozy / welcoming: ζεστός/ή, άνετος, φιλόξενος
- community: γειτονιά, παρέα
- gossip: κουτσομπολιό, κουτσομπολεύω (community care, not malice)
