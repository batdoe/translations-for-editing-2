## Decision findings (details in PROOFREAD_DECISIONS.md)

246. **IT-D1. Title localisation — para 0** — `Capitolo 1` — The title page shows the chapter label 'Capitolo 1' instead of the book title (EN para 0 'Inn Trouble'). The About this Book section itself IS present and translated (heading 28, blurb 30–36). → Proposed: Regenerate the TOC in the formatter (it also needs the 35 missing Heading 1 styles). — DECISION
248. **IT-D3. 7 Scene breaks — para 1181, 1191, 1244** — `***` — English has no `* * *` markers. At these positions the English has only blank spacer paragraphs: EN 917–921 (after the end of ch31, before the 'more mysteries' line), 929–932 (after the What's Next? blurb) and 984–987 (after the excerpt, before the closing 'Flour Play is coming soon!'). → Proposed: Delete the three `***` lines at the formatting stage (or turn them into empty paragraphs) so the edition matches the English. — DECISION
249. **IT-D4. Key clue in English inside a `<bt>` span; `<bt>` on a bakery name — para 910, 651** — `Il biglietto citava le parole di Theo. <bt>The sapphire at its heart</bt>: cinque parole … / sul bancone de <bt>The Holly Jolly Bakery</bt>` — 910: the key clue is printed in ENGLISH mid-sentence and wrapped as if it were a book title. Everywhere else the clue is Italian, and this pass made it uniform as 'lo zaffiro al suo cuore' (177, 178, 195, 807, 808, 809, 948, 1029), which is exactly five words, so the 'cinque parole' remark here stays true. 651: the bakery name is not a book title; 'de The Holly…' is also ungrammatical (1189/1212 say 'la Holly Jolly (Bakery)'). → Proposed: 910: replace `<bt>The sapphire at its heart</bt>` with `Lo zaffiro al suo cuore` (no markers). 651: replace `de <bt>The Holly Jolly Bakery</bt>` with `della Holly Jolly Bakery` (no markers). — DECISION
250. **IT-D5. Italics added by translator — para 633** — `boot camp` — The sweep italicised 'boot camp' (translator's Markdown). The English 'five-thirty boot camp' (EN 494) is roman. 'Boot camp' is a normal fitness loanword in Italian and needs no italics. All 15 English italic runs are present in the Italian. → Proposed: Remove the italic on 'boot camp' to match the English. — DECISION
251. **IT-D6. 5 Dialogue punctuation — whole book** — `«Sì, d'accordo, vieni» le dissi. (ch1) / «Allora ne hai una, ed è già in marcia,» dissi. (ch2) / «Non le piace», confidò Emma (ch6) / «Per me?». 252. **IT-D7. 8 Units (round figures — ESCALATE) — para 185, 1121** — `i più ricchi per cinquanta miglia / il prato misura quattro acri` — 185: 'the richest for fifty miles' is a round, rhetorical figure. 1121: 'the green's four acres'; 'acri' is unusual for Italian readers (4 acres ≈ 1.6 ha).
